The Gap We Close

A cybercriminal needs seconds. A security team needs years.

Most cybersecurity technology deployed in Africa is built in the US, Europe, and Asia. That's not the problem. The problem is what happens next: it takes a partner at least a year to train engineers to truly master a new security platform. An attacker needs seconds to find the gap between what a product can do and what a team knows how to make it do.

The expertise gap

Technology arrives faster than local teams can master it. Every new solution sold without enablement becomes a liability, not an asset.

The reach gap

Global vendors can't build a partner network in every African market from scratch. Emerging vendors have no channel at all.

The support gap

Deployment is not the finish line. Renewals, escalations, and lifecycle management decide whether protection lasts beyond year one.

The Digital City 360° Shield

Six layers. One shield. Complete protection.

Think of every organisation as a digital city — with people, data, applications, and infrastructure that must keep running. Our portfolio is organised into six layers of defense that wrap around that city, protecting it from every angle.

Outer Wall

Network Security

The city's perimeter — firewalls, intrusion prevention, secure access, and DNS protection that keep external threats at the gate.
Sky Layer

Cloud Security

The city's aerial defenses — posture management, workload protection, and cloud-native firewalls across AWS, Azure, and GCP.
Buildings

Endpoint & Workload Security

Every building in the city — laptops, servers, containers — protected with EDR, XDR, and workload defense at the point of impact.
Checkpoints

Identity & Access Management

The city's checkpoints — MFA, privileged access, and Zero Trust controls that verify every citizen before entry.
Command Center

Security Operations

The city's watchtower — SIEM, SOAR, and detection & response that turn raw signals into coordinated action.
Vault

Data Security & Compliance

The city's vault — DLP, encryption, classification, and audit tooling that protect the assets and satisfy regulators.
