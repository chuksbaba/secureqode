The Digital City 360° Shield

Six layers. One shield. Complete protection.

Think of every organisation as a digital city — with people, data, applications, and infrastructure that must keep running. SecureQode's six solution pillars form concentric layers of defense around that city, protecting it from every angle.

Outer Wall

Network Security

The city's outer perimeter — firewalls, intrusion prevention, secure access, and DNS protection that keep external threats at the gate.
Sky Layer

Cloud Security

The city's airborne defenses — posture management, workload protection, and cloud-native firewalls across AWS, Azure, and GCP.
Buildings

Endpoint & Workload Security

Every building in the city — laptops, servers, containers — protected with EDR, XDR, and workload defense at the point of impact.
Checkpoints

Identity & Access Management

The city's access checkpoints — MFA, privileged access, and Zero Trust controls that verify every citizen before entry.
Command Center

Security Operations

The city's watchtower — SIEM, SOAR, and detection & response that turn raw signals into coordinated action.
Vault

Data Security & Compliance

The city's vault — DLP, encryption, classification, and audit tooling that protect the assets and satisfy regulators.
