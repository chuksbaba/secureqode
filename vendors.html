For Vendors

You've built the technology. We'll build the channel.

Selling into African markets from the outside is difficult. The partners are unfamiliar, the buying cycles are different, and the signals that work at home don't necessarily translate here. SecureQode was built to close that distance — giving cybersecurity vendors a real channel without asking them to open a regional office to get one.

Categories We Cover

Six categories. We onboard vendors across all of them.

SecureQode distributes cybersecurity products across six categories. If your product fits one of them and you're serious about building a real African channel, we want to hear from you — whether you're established globally or launching your first partner program.

Network Security

Firewalls, intrusion prevention, secure remote access, DNS protection, and network access control.

Cloud Security

Cloud posture management, workload protection, cloud firewalls, and cloud access security brokers.

Endpoint & Workload

Endpoint protection, EDR, XDR, server security, and mobile threat defense.

Identity & Access

Multi-factor authentication, single sign-on, privileged access, and Zero Trust access controls.

Security Operations

SIEM, SOAR, network detection and response, threat intelligence, and managed detection and response.

Data & Compliance

Data loss prevention, encryption, data classification, backup and recovery, and compliance tooling.

What We Bring

A channel you don't have to assemble yourself.

Every vendor entering a new region faces the same problem set: unfamiliar partners, uncertain demand, different compliance requirements, and no local team to read the market. SecureQode was built to take all of that off your plate — so you can test African demand without betting the company on it.

Partner recruitment, done for you

We identify and screen resellers, MSPs, and integrators who fit your product's profile and the markets you want to reach. Vendors who would normally spend months building a shortlist get one that's already been vetted.

Technical validation and pre-sales support

Our engineers learn your product deeply enough to run demos, support proofs-of-concept, and answer technical objections alongside the partner. You don't have to fly a team in for every opportunity.

Enablement that sticks

We train partners on your product — not just what it does, but how to sell it, position it against alternatives, and support it after go-live. Enablement that produces partners who can actually close.

Demand generation with local context

Joint campaigns, regional events, and lead programs that reflect how business is actually done in the markets you're entering — not a translated version of what worked somewhere else.

Commercial flexibility for channel realities

Payment terms, licensing structures, and subscription billing designed around the working-capital realities of African channel businesses. Deals that fit the market, not deals that fit an ideal balance sheet.

Renewals managed as carefully as new business

We track renewals, manage escalations, and support expansion conversations. A first deal becomes a multi-year revenue stream, not a one-off transaction that quietly evaporates.

Emerging vendors get real attention

If you're an emerging vendor without an established channel program, you get the same infrastructure a large vendor would — partner recruitment, technical validation, demand generation — without having to first prove yourself to a distributor's tiering committee.

Start the Conversation

We're actively onboarding vendors across all six cybersecurity categories. Whether you're established globally and new to the region, or an emerging player looking for a first channel — tell us about your product and your goals, and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.
