Vendor Portfolio

Bring your cybersecurity product to Africa.

SecureQode specialises in onboarding cybersecurity vendors — particularly those new to the African market — and building the channel infrastructure, technical validation, and demand generation needed to grow a sustainable regional business.

Portfolio Categories

Six categories. One distribution partner.

Our vendor portfolio is organised around the Digital City 360° Shield. We onboard vendors across every category below — and we actively look for emerging players with strong technology and a clear African opportunity.

Network Security Vendors

Firewalls, IPS/IDS, secure web gateways, DNS security, and network access control.

Cloud Security Vendors

CSPM, CWPP, CNAPP, CASB, and cloud-native firewall platforms.

Endpoint & Workload Vendors

EDR, XDR, EPP, server and container security, and mobile threat defense.

Identity & Access Vendors

IAM, MFA, PAM, SSO, ZTNA, and identity governance platforms.

Security Operations Vendors

SIEM, SOAR, NDR, threat intelligence, MDR, and vulnerability management.

Data & Compliance Vendors

DLP, encryption, data classification, backup and recovery, and compliance tooling.

Why Vendors Choose SecureQode

We build the channel you don't have yet.

Entering a new region is hard. The partners are unfamiliar, the compliance landscape is different, and the demand signals are difficult to read from a distance. SecureQode removes those barriers for cybersecurity vendors who want to grow in Africa.

Market entry without the overhead

You don't need to open an office, hire a regional team, or build a partner program from zero. We provide the local presence, the relationships, and the operational infrastructure.

Partner recruitment and enablement

We identify, recruit, and enable resellers, MSPs, and integrators who match your ideal partner profile — and we train them on your product so they can sell it with confidence.

Technical validation and pre-sales support

Our engineers understand your technology well enough to run demonstrations, support POCs, and answer technical objections alongside the partner.

Demand generation that reaches real buyers

Joint marketing campaigns, regional events, and targeted lead generation programs that connect your product to the African organisations that need it.

Recurring revenue through renewals

We manage renewals, subscriptions, and consumption tracking so that a successful first deal becomes an ongoing revenue stream — not a one-off sale.
