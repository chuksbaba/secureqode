Services 1st

Distribution is the vehicle. Services are the value.

SecureQode is a value-added distributor — which means we're measured by what our partners can do after working with us, not by how many boxes we shipped. Every service below exists to make our partners more capable, more competitive, and more profitable.

Pre-Sales & Solution Design

Technical validation, architecture design, sizing, and proof-of-concept support. Our engineers work alongside yours to make sure the recommended solution actually fits the customer's environment.

Partner Enablement & Training

Structured training paths, vendor certifications, and hands-on labs that raise the technical capability of every partner — from sales teams to engineers.

Marketing & Demand Generation

Co-branded campaigns, lead generation programs, and regional events that fill partner pipelines with qualified opportunities and shorten sales cycles.

Logistics & Fulfillment

Regional stocking, order management, and delivery across African markets. We handle the operational complexity so partners can focus on the customer.

Financial Solutions

Flexible payment terms, subscription and consumption billing, and renewal management designed to support partner cash flow and recurring revenue.

Post-Sales & Lifecycle Support

Renewals, technical escalation, and long-term lifecycle management. We stay involved after the deal closes so partners keep their customers protected.

How It Works

A services-led approach at every stage

Most distributors sell products and leave the rest to their partners. SecureQode inverts that model. We wrap services around every product we distribute, from the moment a partner first engages a customer to the moment a renewal is due.

1. Before the sale

Our pre-sales engineers help partners qualify opportunities, design the right architecture, and run proof-of-concepts. We don't hand over a datasheet — we help build a solution.

2. During the deal

We support partner pricing, licensing structures, and deal registration. Financial flexibility, including payment terms and subscription billing, is available to qualified partners.

3. After delivery

Our post-sales team supports implementation questions, escalations, and renewal tracking. We treat every deployment as the start of a long-term relationship, not the end of a transaction.

4. Across the lifecycle

Marketing programs, enablement, and technical resources are available continuously — so partners keep growing their cybersecurity practice, not just their product catalog.
