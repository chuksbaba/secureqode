The Partner Journey

From first conversation to long-term partnership

We don't sign partners and hand them a portal login. We work with you to build a cybersecurity practice that grows — one deal, one enablement session, one renewal at a time.

1. First conversation

We learn your business — the customers you serve, the solutions you already sell, and the gaps in your portfolio. Then we recommend which parts of the Digital City 360° Shield make sense for you to lead with.

2. Onboarding and enablement

We train your sales and technical teams on the solutions you'll be selling. No canned slide decks — hands-on labs, real demo environments, and access to engineers who've deployed the product before.

3. First opportunities

We work alongside you on your first deals — pre-sales engineering support, pricing guidance, and POC coordination. Our goal is to help you win before you're fully independent.

4. Scale and renew

As you close more deals, we stay involved — supporting renewals, escalating technical issues, and feeding you qualified leads. The partnership grows as your practice grows.
